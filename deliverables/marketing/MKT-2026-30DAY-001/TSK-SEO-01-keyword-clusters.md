## 1. Executive SEO Strategy Overview

Modern AI software ranking algorithms (Google Search, Perplexity, ChatGPT Search, Gemini) prioritize **semantic completeness, verifiable technical architecture, and deep intent resolution** over legacy keyword stuffing.

Our strategy targets **high-intent, lower-competition long-tail search phrases** across four distinct intent pillars:
1. **Pillar 1: Commercial / Solution-Seeking** (High conversion intent; founders seeking autonomous agent tools)
2. **Pillar 2: Technical / Architectural** (Engineers looking for local, zero-cost, self-hosted LLM systems)
3. **Pillar 3: Comparative / Alternative** (Users seeking alternatives to expensive cloud agent SaaS)
4. **Pillar 4: Compliance & Safety** (Enterprises demanding human-in-the-loop auditability & kill-switches)
