## 2. Minimum Human Interaction Workflow

All routine operational tasks—topic research, keyword mapping, copy generation, schema validation, and calendar scheduling—are 100% automated by the AI Command Center.

Human intervention is restricted exclusively to **7 Sensitive Governance Gates**:
1. **Account Creation:** Registering accounts on YouTube, Gumroad, Lemon Squeezy, or Medium.
2. **Legal Agreements:** Accepting terms of service and affiliate contracts.
3. **Financial Credentials:** Setting up banking, PayPal, or Stripe payout details.
4. **Physical Recording:** Speaking into a microphone or webcam for YouTube video content.
5. **Final Click-to-Publish:** Hitting the final "Publish" button on third-party platforms.
6. **Payout Approvals:** Withdrawing funds to a bank account.
7. **Emergency Halt:** Activating the distributed kill-switch if any external metric behaves unexpectedly.

## 3. Financial Integrity & Reporting Standards
- **Zero Fabricated Revenue:** The system records exactly `$0.00` until verified sales receipts or affiliate conversion reports are confirmed.
- **Deduplication:** Transactions are reconciled using unique transaction IDs and platform order IDs to prevent double-counting.
- **Reconciliation Statuses:** `PENDING` (Order placed, awaiting settlement) ➔ `CONFIRMED` (Past refund window) ➔ `PAID_OUT` (Deposited in owner bank account).
